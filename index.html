Maintenance Advice

Footwear Care & Longevity

Extend the lifespan and pristine appearance of your shoes with these practical maintenance guidelines.

1. Proper Cleaning

Use soft bristle brushes for suede/canvas and specialized cream polish for full-grain leather.

2. Air Drying

Always air-dry damp shoes naturally at room temperature. Avoid direct sunlight or heavy artificial heat.

3. Storage & Trees

Store shoes in breathable dust bags or shoe trees to maintain original crease-free structure.

4. Sole Protection

Keep outsoles clean of debris to preserve traction and prevent premature tread wear.
